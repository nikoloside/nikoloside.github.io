ABOUT

My work sits between games, AI agents, and computer graphics research. Some of it is published research on physics-based animation; some is shipping, playable systems where autonomous agents drive narrative and play. WorldLines is where those two threads meet — an attempt to build worlds that remember.

If we are standing at the early stage of agent-native games, I want to see that landscape clearly — and build the tools that let more people make worlds of their own.
